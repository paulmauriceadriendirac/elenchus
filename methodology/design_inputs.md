## The human's instructions (claude.ai conversation with Claude Opus 5.5, 21 October 2026; paraphrased)

1. Build a harness, to be run in Claude Code, that produces one of the entrant's (up to three)
   competition essays; make the approach novel, useful and enlightening, and aim to win.
2. Use the public information about the judging panel — their areas and interests — so that the
   essay lands where the judges can evaluate it.
3. Cost is not a constraint; prioritize the quality of the essays.
4. Make sure the human cannot accidentally supply ideas.
5. Generic methods the human suggested: consult PhilPapers Survey results and prize-winning work
   to calibrate what philosophers find interesting or contested; consider metaphilosophy and
   experimental philosophy on how philosophy is done; look at existing work on AI essay
   generation.
6. Ask the human to fetch any page, text or PDF the agents cannot access.

## What the scaffold is

- Designed and written by Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`) in that conversation, which also
  researched the competition's rules and FAQ and the Claude Code documentation.
- It contains process, quality standards, the published judging criteria (paraphrased), generic
  philosophical methods, and software tools. It contains no philosophical topics, theses,
  arguments, cases or reading lists. Topic selection happens inside the run, by agents.
