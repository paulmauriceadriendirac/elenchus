## 1. The package: steelman

1. Normality is the received way to tell structurally isomorphic cases apart: bogus prevention vs overdetermination, short circuit vs preemption, switch vs preemption.
2. The anchors' implementations apply normality to background facts (A&G's deviancy condition) or to whole witness worlds (Halpern–Hitchcock, "HH"). Either way, they classify a deviant event that preempts a normally running process (the locusts preempting a dry-season drought) the way they classify a switch. On that basis they deny that the event caused the outcome.
3. Within a natural class of typicality rankings, the only rankings that keep HP's preemption, prevention and short-circuit verdicts penalize exactly unlicensed deviance. None of them separates switching from preempting a normal process. So switch verdicts must come from model aptness, and normality's job in HP is a determinate "licence" rule rather than a free dial.

That is the version attacked below.

## 2. Card by card

### A4-01: Locusts (normal-backup preemption)

**Steelman.** A deviant event preempts a backup process whose only enablers are normal absences (no rain in the dry season). Both A&G theories, and HP with HH's stated typicality guidelines, then deny that the event is a cause, although every normality-free account counts it a cause. Flipping only the backup trigger's normality restores "cause", so the verdict tracks the normality of a causally idle condition.

**Hand check of the encodings: correct.**
- NBP1. L′ must contain both laws, because D and E are descendants of Lo.
  - So E ↔ Lo ∨ ¬R, and the unique maximal F′ that does not entail E is {¬D}. Condition (3) holds.
  - ¬R ∈ F∖F′ is a normal root, neither ancestor nor descendant of Lo. So PPQ condition (4) fails, and so does the Phil Stud condition (v).
  - Beckers 2026 states (v) as AG7's (C5): "for any literal C′ ∈ V∖V′ whose variable is neither a descendant nor an ancestor of C, C′ is more deviant than ¬C′". He proves that AG7 is AG5 with C1/C2 in place of D1/D2. This matches the code's `'2024b'` branch.
- Modified HP without normality: the witness [Lo←0, D←0] makes Lo a cause.
- NESS: {Lo=1} is sufficient for E and ∅ is not. CNESS: Lo=0 NESS-causes E only along (D), not along the empty path. So Lo is a CNESS cause. These agree with the card.

**Objections**

1. **Already known (Andreas–Günther half). Severity: fatal to that half; serious overall.**
   - Beckers 2026 argues that A&G face a dilemma between structural and deviancy-based treatments of the switch. On the deviancy horn, "all of their accounts offer the wrong verdict on cases of Early Preemption for which the backup-process is not deviant" (p. 2).
   - His "Backup 2" is a trainee surgeon preempting a norm-conforming supervisor. He concludes that AG5, AG6 and AG7 "force us to conclude that trainee's action did not cause the outcome" (p. 11).
   - He also proves AG7 ⇔ AG5 (Thm 2.2). So the locusts verdict carries over to A&G's current APR 2025 account, which the card never mentions.
   - Best reply: the locusts make the backup normal under every criterion A&G use (it is an absence, statistically normal, and violates no norm). Beckers's case instead turns on a moral norm making an occurrence non-deviant. Proposition 1 generalizes the case. **Partial.**
   - Repair: present the A&G result as Beckers's, with the locusts and Proposition 1 as a robustness strengthening. Do not put any novelty claim here.

2. **Currency. Severity: serious until checked.**
   - A&G's target has moved: APR 9(2) 2025, with commentaries by Hall, Hitchcock, Weslake, Wysocki and Ackermans and an A&G reply [abs].
   - A 2025 talk reportedly refines deviancy into an "inferential" and a "weak" notion [abs]. A book, *From Reasons to Causes*, is forthcoming [abs].
   - Reply: Beckers's equivalence covers AG7. The 2025 refinement and the book are unread. **Partial.**
   - Repair: read the symposium (Hitchcock's and Hall's commentaries first), the reply, and whatever states the refined deviancy notion.

3. **Already known, risk (HH half). Severity: serious.**
   - Harinen 2017 argues [abs] that HH's way of ruling out bogus prevention leaves their account unable to handle cases "in which the actual effect is normal".
   - Proposition 2's hypothesis ("E occurs in the all-default solution") just says the effect is normal, and the dry-season destruction is a normal effect.
   - The principle is in Halpern's own words. On bogus prevention he writes: "We might argue that we don't need a cause for an event that was expected all along. Here normality considerations can help." (Halpern 2015, Ex. 3.4; verified in the arXiv PDF).
   - Reply: unknown until Harinen is read. Repair: read Harinen (fetch already filed per the card). If his counterexamples are preemptions of normal processes, the HH half is anticipated in substance.

4. **Premise the audience rejects (HH's practice is value-level in preemption). Severity: serious.**
   - HH's own preemption analysis judges the frozen backup by its value, not by conformity to its equation. In the witness, B=0 although B = 1−A requires B=1. HH write: "It seems natural to take A = 0 and B = 0 to be typical" (arXiv 1309.1226, p. 34).
   - Applied to the locusts, the same value-level practice makes D=0 (the maize not dying) a typical value, and the locusts come out a cause.
   - HH call their equation-typicality assumption defeasible: "unless there is some specific reason to think otherwise" (p. 19). They say they offer "a flexible framework" rather than the "right" ordering (fn. 13, p. 26). They allow cross-variable comparisons when "special considerations" warrant (p. 20; §7.6).
   - Best reply:
     - HH state the rationale for equation-typicality: "to ensure that the downstream consequences of what is typical are themselves typical" (p. 19). In the locusts witness all roots take typical values, so by that rationale the drought death is typical there.
     - HH override this in preemption only because the backup act is "morally wrong, unlawful, and highly unusual" (p. 34). No such override is available for a drought killing unwatered maize.
     - A cross-variable trade-off would make "did these locusts destroy this maize?" depend on whether locust swarms are rarer than maize surviving a dry week.
   - Reply quality: **partial to good.**
   - Repair: drop the "Default Rules 1–2" framing. Attack the conflict between HH's two practices instead: value-level typicality for preemption (§7.7), and conditional or equation-level typicality for the switch (Halpern 2015, Ex. 3.9). That conflict is A4-04, repaired.

5. **Scope and overclaim. Severity: serious.**
   - The card claims that "normality stops counting as a solved add-on" and that every normality-based account owes an answer. This is overstated.
   - Gallow 2021 says C caused E when "both C and E are deviant or noninertial events, and there is an uninterrupted process which transmits C's deviancy to E" (p. 47). On my reading of his departure/return conditions (pp. 85–88; §§3–5 not re-read), he counts the locusts a cause as long as the destruction is coded deviant.
   - Hitchcock 2007 probably does the same; it is unread.
   - A 2023 Synthese paper argues [abs] that normality ascriptions fail to resolve isomorphs, because conjoining isomorphs yields cases they cannot resolve. The general moral may already be in print.
   - Repair: scope the claim to normality used as a filter on excluded background facts or on whole witness worlds. Mention that under normal-course coding of the effect, Gallow's productive theory may also deny the locusts; check this before asserting it.

6. **Invalid inference (Proposition 1, Phil Stud version). Severity: moderate.**
   - The proof asserts that the excluded n ∈ N is "neither ancestor nor descendant of C". Roots are never descendants, but they can be ancestors of an endogenous C, and (v)/(C5) exempts ancestors.
   - Repair: add "no member of N is an ancestor of C". The locust models satisfy this because Lo is a root.

7. **Coding escape for A&G. Severity: moderate.**
   - Recode the trigger as an occurrence ("drought"). Under A&G's prima facie rule that occurrences are more deviant than absences, the drought then counts as deviant and Lo becomes a cause.
   - Reply: A&G themselves tie occurrence vs absence to norms ("Is, for example, the death of a plant an occurring event, or rather the absence of continued living?", PPQ p. 26). The same lexical move ("poison-free coffee" as a state) would undo their bogus-prevention solution. **Convincing.** Keep it in the essay.

8. **Is the verdict firm? Severity: moderate.**
   - Abnormal-deflation findings (Icard, Kominsky & Knobe 2017) and Harinen's correspondence hypothesis [abs] predict weaker ratings for an abnormal factor when the outcome was normally on its way.
   - Reply: lean on the production verdict ("the locusts destroyed the maize"). HH's AC2+(a) yields outright non-causation, not a lower grade. **Partial to good.** Repair: say plainly that the verdict concerns production, and do not claim it is "as firm as any preemption" without data.

**Verdict: REPAIR, folded into the recommended essay.** Confidence 0.6. It becomes KILL (about 0.3 probability) if Harinen 2017 already runs normal-backup preemption against HH.

### A4-02: "Deviations need licences" (a characterization within HH)

**Steelman.**
- Class: local, uniform typicality rankings, i.e. a variable's typicality depends only on (default value?, obeys own equation?), with one ranking shared by all variables and worlds compared variable by variable as in HH's p. 20 guideline.
- Four canonical cases force a single admissible content: penalize a value only if it is a deviant value its own mechanism did not produce.
- That turns normality from a per-case dial into a rule, and isolates the two verdicts (switch, mentor) that normality cannot deliver.

**Hand checks**
- 2,071 = (4683 − 541)/2: weak orders on 6 items with R0 above R1. Correct.
- Comparisons are only ever within one variable. So where the R-categories sit relative to the E-categories never matters, and only the 75 weak orders on {E00, E01, E10, E11} behave differently.
- The stated constraints (E10 = E00, E01 ≥ E00, E11 below all) admit exactly 2 of those 75:
  - E11 < E00 = E10 = E01
  - E11 < E00 = E10 < E01
- The reported 38 equals 25 + 13 placements of R0 > R1 around those two orders. So the output is internally consistent with the characterization.
- The necessity cases check out by hand:
  - genuine prevention forces E10 ≥ E00;
  - chain or overdetermination forces E00 ≥ E10;
  - modified-HP witnesses for preemption and for endogenous causes force E01 ≥ E00;
  - the short circuit forces E11 < E10.

**Objections**

1. **Inflated, or trivial once made precise. Severity: moderate.**
   - "Exhaustive enumeration of 2,071 schemes" is in fact a choice among 75 orders with 2 survivors, which a hand proof can settle. Experts will read the 2,071 as padding.
   - Repair: report 75 and 2, and give the hand proof.

2. **Overclaim and stale numbers. Severity: serious.**
   - The thesis says the admissible schemes are those "that give the standard verdicts on a 24-query battery". The code's `HB` has 32 queries (MENTOR, ISOMEDP, DEVOM, MENTOR_N, BP_N, GP_N and SW_LBRB were added). By the card's own report, no scheme gets them all.
   - The characterization picks out near-misses, with SW and MENTOR excluded after the fact.
   - Repair: state a necessity theorem from the four forcing cases, which is clean and holds regardless of battery size. Present sufficiency as relative to the battery, and justify excluding SW and MENTOR independently, by aptness.

3. **Premise HH reject. Severity: serious.**
   - HH disclaim rule-generated orderings (fn. 13).
   - They use mechanism-specific dependence rankings: P "= 0 regardless of A" ranked above "P = A" (§7.7, p. 35).
   - They use typical behaviour decoupled from the equation in the dam footnote: "even though the actual behavior of the dam does not depend upon the rain, its typical behavior does" (fn. 18, p. 36).
   - They use cross-variable typicality in §7.6.
   - The local-uniform class excludes all three.
   - Reply: the class formalizes HH's stated default (p. 19 assumption, p. 20 guideline). The result says what the default must be if exceptions are to remain exceptions. **Partial.**

4. **Already known (likely). Severity: serious.**
   - The licence rule reads like a typicality version of the default/deviant idea in Hitchcock 2007 (deviance needs a deviant source) and Hall 2007 (default-world reductions); both unread.
   - HH's own preemption practice already treats a frozen default as typical, which amounts to E01 ≥ E00.
   - Repair: read Hitchcock 2007 and Hall 2007. Pitch the increment as "within HH's witness framework, the Hitchcock-2007-style rule is forced", and only if the reading confirms it is not already said.

5. **Coding fragility. Severity: serious.**
   - Battery verdicts depend on default codings; the code codes every variable's default as 0 except DEVOM.
   - With the train's left track coded as default (LT = 1), the licence rule gets Hall's switch right (see A4-04). So "every admissible scheme counts the flip a cause" is coding-relative.
   - Repair: give a coding-robustness table (event/unconditional, inertial, normal-course).

**Verdict: REPAIR, as the positive half of the recommended essay; not a standalone essay.** Confidence 0.6.

### A4-03: The frozen-witness lemma

**Steelman.**
- A modified-HP witness can only freeze actual values. So any witness for a cause the effect does not depend on counterfactually, or for an endogenous candidate, must contain an equation violation.
- Hence, under HH's equation-typicality default, the modified definition counts only root-level but-for conjunctions as causes: Billy's preempted throw becomes part of the cause, and no intermediate event is ever a cause.
- HH's remedy of adding variables (§7.7) cannot help, because modified witnesses never reset roots.

The lemma and its corollaries are correct (checked by hand on late preemption and on a two-link chain).

**Objections**

1. **Trivial once made precise. Severity: serious for a standalone essay.**
   - If no variable violates its equation, frozen values coincide with their counterfactual values, so the witness world is the solution under [X←x′] alone, which is but-for dependence.
   - For an endogenous X, the parents keep their actual values, so x′ contradicts X's equation.
   - That is two lines.

2. **Misdescription. Severity: moderate.**
   - HH do not "raise a question" in §7.7. They assert: "We can avoid this by adding an additional variable to the model" (p. 36), in a context where the original definition is in force.
   - The correct increment: that remedy is unavailable under the modified definition. Halpern 2015 says the HH extension "applies without change to the modified definition" (Ex. 3.4; verified).

3. **Straw combination. Severity: serious.**
   - No one runs the modified definition with exceptionless equation-typicality. HH already suspend it in their preemption example, and Halpern 2015 Ex. 3.10 judges value combinations ("directly contradicting the story").
   - Reply: the lemma shows the suspension must be wholesale, not occasional, under the modified definition. **Partial.** It is worth one paragraph.

4. **Already known, risk.** Halpern 2016, ch. 3 (unread) may treat normality under AC2(a^m) explicitly.

**Verdict: KILL as a card. Keep it as a lemma (one paragraph) in the recommended essay.** Confidence 0.85.

### A4-04: The switch is not a normality problem

**Steelman.**
- In HP the switch flip's witness freezes the untaken route at its default against its mechanism.
- Normal-backup preemption needs exactly that kind of frozen default to count as typical; late preemption and every endogenous cause under the modified definition need it too.
- So no typicality ranking in the class can deny the flip while keeping these verdicts. Normality cannot supply the switch verdict, and the switch is an aptness question.

**Hand check (code's coding, LT default 0)**
- The modified witness for F is [F←0, LT←0]. LT=0 there is a default value that violates its equation (category E01). RT, A and F are all at least as typical as in the actual world.
- So the witness passes iff E01 ≥ E00. The locusts need E01 ≥ E00, and the switch needs E01 < E00.
- The minimal unsatisfiable pair {NBP, SW} is therefore correct as coded.

**Objections**

1. **Coding escape. Severity: serious (breaks the result as stated).**
   - Code the default route as default: LT = 1, because the train goes left unless the switch is flipped. Then the frozen LT=0 is deviant and unlicensed (E11), which the short-circuit constraint already makes atypical.
   - So the licence rule gets the switch right, while the locusts, with the drought death coded 0, stay right.
   - The card checked "arrival is the default" but not "left track is the default".
   - Repair: replace or supplement Hall's train with **Gallow's Doorbells** (pp. 84–86), where both routes are events: a bell ringing (L = M ∧ ¬D, R = M ∧ D, E = L ∨ R).
     - On any unconditional or inertial coding, the left bell's default is 0, and the flip's witness freezes L=0 against M=1, D=0 (E01). That is the same relation as the locusts witness.
     - Coding the untaken route as default by the normal course of events, given the actual triggers, rescues Doorbells but codes the dry-season drought death as default too. The locusts witness then has D=0 at E11 against an actual E10, and is blocked by the short circuit.
     - Hand check: under each uniform coding rule (event, inertial, normal course from all-default roots, normal course given actual triggers), at least one of {Doorbells, locusts, short circuit, modified-HP late/early preemption} fails for every local uniform ranking.

2. **Already known. Severity: serious.**
   - Halpern 2015 Ex. 3.9 handles two of the three switch models by model choice: the three-variable F/T/A model ("all three definitions agree that flipping the switch is not a cause"), and the LB/RB model under the modified definition ("a non-problem").
   - He invokes normality only for Hall's LT/RT model: "the world where the train does not go on the left track despite the switch being set to the left is less normal than the actual world" (verified).
   - HH 2010 treat that model as inapt (per A&G). Beckers 2026 gives the A&G analogue of the switch/early-preemption dilemma.
   - So "the switch is an aptness question" is half-known. The increment is narrower: Halpern's normality move for LT/RT conflicts with HH's value-level preemption practice, so it must go.
   - **Partial.**

3. **Trivial once made precise. Severity: moderate.**
   - The conflict reduces to one relation (E01 vs E00).
   - Reply: that is the point of a normality-isomorph: two cases whose witness worlds have the same typicality profile but opposite verdicts. The essay's weight must rest on the case analysis and the coding robustness. **Partial.**

4. **Scope.** Same as A4-02, objection 3 (non-local, non-uniform orderings).

5. **Prior art, risk.** The Synthese 2023 isomorph paper [abs] and Blanchard & Schaffer 2017 [abs] may already contain "isomorphs that share their defaults".

**Verdict: REPAIR, as the core of the recommended essay.** Confidence 0.6.

### A4-05: Verdicts hostage to idle absences

**Steelman.** In A&G, whether c caused e flips with the normative status of an absence that makes no difference: the locusts, and Paul & Hall's MEDP. The cause is that (iii) uses unrestricted entailment, so (v) tests facts linked to e only backwards through C's own effects. A forward-candidate restriction fixes the locusts at the cost of MEDP alone.

**Objections**

1. **Proves too much. Severity: fatal.**
   - Take bogus prevention: E = ¬F ∧ D, with F=1 and D=0. The absence ¬D is idle in the card's sense: had poison been added, the victim would still have survived.
   - On A&G, both versions, F is a cause of ¬E iff ¬D is more deviant than D. HH's account behaves the same way.
   - The card's invariance principle therefore condemns normality's solution to bogus prevention itself.
   - No credible reply: the flip is the intended function of normality.

2. **The alleged defect is arguably correct. Severity: serious.**
   - If G's absence in MEDP violates a norm, then G is expected, and with G present the threat B would exist independently of F. F's neutralizer C is then a genuine preventer relative to the normal background, so "cause" is defensible.
   - Paul & Hall's reason, as A&G report it ("this non-occurrence does not make any difference", PPQ p. 22), concerns the normal case.

3. **The repair's cost. Severity: serious.**
   - Hand check: with F′ = {A, B}, ¬G is not a forward candidate, because B's law is removed. So the repaired theory makes f a cause in MEDP whatever G's status. That loses A&G's showcase case.
   - The quoted rationale, "in the presence of the laws L′" (Phil Stud p. 2158, not verified by me), describes unrestricted entailment, not forward entailment. So it does not support the repair.

4. **Already known.** Beckers 2026 §4 already presses A&G's "flip-flopping" between structural and deviancy-based verdicts.

**Verdict: KILL.** Confidence 0.8. At most, the "backward inference through C's own effects" diagnosis can be a footnote.

## 6. Recommended essay: merge A4-04 + A4-02 + A4-03, with A4-01's case

**Working title:** "Normality Cannot Tell Switching from Preempting."

**Calibrated thesis**
- Setting: Halpern–Pearl definitions with Halpern–Hitchcock witness normality.
- Claim: no typicality ranking that judges each variable by (i) whether its value is its default and (ii) whether that value conforms to its own mechanism, compared variable by variable, can both
  - count a deviant event that preempts a normally running process as a cause (the locusts), and
  - deny that a switch flip which merely reroutes an outcome causes it (Doorbells),

  under any uniform rule for assigning defaults (event, inertial, or normal course), without also losing short circuits or preemption under the modified definition.
- Positive half: the only such rankings that keep the standard preemption, prevention and short-circuit verdicts are the licence rankings (penalize only deviant values not produced by the variable's own mechanism).
- Consequence: switch verdicts must come from model aptness, as Halpern already secures them for two of his three switch models, and his normality treatment of Hall's LT/RT model must go.
- Not claimed: that normality is idle; that no non-local ordering can work; that Gallow's account fails.

**Closest prior work, and the exact increment**
- **Beckers 2026.** He established the switch/early-preemption dilemma for A&G's deviancy accounts, and AG7 ⇔ AG5. Increment: the HH analogue, with proof and coding robustness.
- **HH 2015 §§6, 7.7, fn. 18; Halpern 2015 Ex. 3.4 and 3.9.** These give the equation-typicality default and its exceptions, value-level preemption practice versus conditional switch practice, and the add-a-variable remedy. Increment:
  - the two practices cannot be unified (Doorbells/locusts);
  - under the modified definition the default must be dropped wholesale (lemma);
  - the licence rule is forced within the class.
- **Hitchcock 2007 and Hall 2007** (to be read): the licence rule may be their idea in HH's terms. If so, the increment is that it is forced within HH's framework, plus its limit.
- **Harinen 2017** [abs]: HH fail on normal effects. Increment, if it survives reading: the exact mechanism, and its link to the switch.
- **Synthese 2023** [abs], **Blanchard & Schaffer 2017** [abs]: normality versus isomorphs. Increment: a specific natural pair, robust to coding. Must be checked.
- **Gallow 2021:** Doorbells. His deviancy constraints sit on cause, effect and departure/return variables. Use as the contrast case.

**Three objections the essay must answer**
1. **Flexibility and non-locality.**
   - Objection: HH's orderings need not be local or uniform. Mechanism-specific rankings (§7.7), typical-behaviour functions (fn. 18), or cross-variable "special considerations" (p. 20, §7.6) could save both verdicts.
   - Answer:
     - Show what any saving ordering must do: make the typicality of a frozen default depend on a feature that does not differ between Doorbells and the locusts, or trade swarm rarity against drought survival. The latter makes the verdict hostage to irrelevant base rates.
     - Use the lemma to show that under the modified definition the exceptions would have to be universal.
2. **Coding.**
   - Objection: defaults are free. Make the default route or bell the default, or use inertial coding.
   - Answer: Doorbells plus the analysis across the four uniform coding rules. Lexical recoding cuts equally against the bogus-prevention solution.
3. **The data.**
   - Objection: (a) switch models with route variables are inapt anyway (HH 2010), so no conflict arises; (b) the locusts verdict is soft (abnormal deflation; Harinen's correspondence hypothesis).
   - Answer (a): accept aptness as the conclusion, while showing that Halpern's normality treatment of LT/RT and A&G's deviancy treatment (via Beckers) are thereby idle or wrong.
   - Answer (b): the verdict concerns production ("destroyed"), and AC2+(a) delivers outright non-causation, not a lower grade.

**Feasibility and risk**
- Feasible in 6,000 words: two cases, one lemma, one forced-ranking theorem with a hand proof, one impossibility corollary, and a coding table.
- Significance is moderate but legible: normality was brought in to sort isomorphs, and here is a natural pair it cannot sort.
- I estimate about a 50% chance that it survives the remaining prior-art reading (Harinen; Halpern 2016, ch. 3; Synthese 2023).

## 7. Code audit (`A4_models.py`, read, not executed)

**Faithful**

  - original: AC2(b) over subsets Z′ with W fully set;
