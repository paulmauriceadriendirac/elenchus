## 2. What changed and why
- **Thesis replaced.** v2 said extensionality is "neither disguised internalism nor bedrock". v3 says extensionality is CAR for the evaluator's learning model (transparency in distribution). With opaque problems counted, it holds only on a knife-edge maintained by the standard's symmetry. So the argument rests on a neutrality requirement on evaluation, which an externalist can accept only by abstracting from systematic opacity.
- **New results** (all short, all checked): Prop 1 (weightings = coarsening mechanisms; Lemma 1 as Greaves–Wallace in the report space); Prop 4 (knife-edge: every reweighting of support Σ is extensional iff Σ is transparent; adding any weight to one opaque problem breaks extensionality); Prop 5 (neutrality ⇒ extensional; sufficient, not necessary); Prop 6 (extensional ⇔ induced per-proposition scores strictly proper); Prop 7 (No News ⇔ λ_S constant for every weighting; separable profiles: ext ⇔ No News ⇔ A constant, with g forced by the budget); the Zendejas Medina relation (§6); minimax regret (Obj. 3).
- **Kept:** Prop 2 (uniform = 2^{(n−1)^2} × yes/no; random colouring), Prop 3 (λ°), Enough Butter, questions-vs-answers corollary, maximin.
- **Dropped:** v2's representative-agent argument for extensionality (circular); "verdict has no internalist equivalent"; "nothing more" in Obj. 2; the Isaacs–Russell and Das attributions (unread; Das is no longer cited).
- **Concessions in text:** selection-equivalence (§3.3); approximate stability under small perturbations (Obj. 2); neutrality not mandatory and blind to state structure (§4, §8).

## 3. v3 thesis (one sentence)
Meehan and Zhang's extensionality is coarsening at random imposed on the learning model implicit in the evaluator's weighting: weaker than counting only transparent problems, but, with opaque problems counted, satisfied only on a knife-edge held in place by the symmetry of the standard. So the total-accuracy vindication of conditionalization rests on a neutrality requirement on evaluation, not on internalism, and binds externalists only if evaluation should abstract from the systematic ways evidence fails to be transparent.

## 4. Increment
- **Over Meehan & Zhang:**
  - the identification of weightings with coarsening mechanisms and of extensionality with CAR (their Lemma 2 and Fact 12 recognized as CAR facts);
  - the uniform total = random yes/no questions (Fact 10 = Greaves–Wallace summed);
  - the knife-edge proposition (sharpening their "strong connection", 338);
  - neutrality and propriety as criteria for extensionality that do not presuppose BCOND (bearing on their "bedrock" question, 337);
  - the separable-importance/No News theorem;
  - the exact content of their high-level/low-level suggestion (338);
  - the failure of maximin, minimax regret and dominance.
- **Over the CAR literature:** no new statistics. The contributions are the transfer to accuracy-first epistemology (evaluator's weighting as coarsening mechanism; Hi-TEA as sophisticated conditioning in the report space) and what follows for the externalism debate: the agent-level reading of Schoenfield's divergence as non-CAR learning; the knife-edge (support-relative) formulation in weighting terms; neutrality and propriety; and the opaque-only uniform standard λ° as a natural, non-contrived CAR standard outside the convex hull of partitions. The fragility of non-partition CAR mechanisms is credited to G&G (fair-coin remark) and G&H ("holds rather infrequently").

## 5. Three weakest remaining points
1. **Formal modesty.** Props 1 and 4–7 are short consequences of Lemma 1, Fact 12, linearity and the budget identity. The value is the reframing. A specialist may judge the agent-level moral (§6) to be implicit in the CAR literature, and the essay concedes the statistics.
2. **The neutrality argument is not conclusive.** §4 states its cost (it ignores the structure of the state space, which is where margin-for-error opacity comes from), and the conclusion does not claim it is mandatory. "Who can accept the argument" is therefore conditional, and the philosophical payoff depends on readers finding the relocation illuminating.
3. **Thin engagement with the wider externalist literature.** Gallow 2021, Das, Bronfman 2014 and Isaacs & Russell are not engaged, because I did not read them. Carr 2021 and Zendejas Medina 2024 are engaged only through MZ's reports (and an abstract). The §6 claim that the divergence of BCOND and META-COND requires non-CAR learning may already appear in that literature. Fetch requests would be the next step.
