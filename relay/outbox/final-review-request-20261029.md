# Request for a defect-finding review (paste with one essay PDF attached)

You are reviewing an anonymous philosophy research paper (attached). Do not give an overall
opinion, a score, or stylistic advice. Report only specific defects, each with evidence, of
these three kinds:

1. **Formal error.** A stated theorem, proposition, lemma or worked example that is false, or a
   proof that is invalid or relies on an assumption the paper does not state. Give the location,
   the exact problem, and a counterexample or the missing assumption.
2. **Misrepresented source.** A passage where the paper attributes to a cited work something it
   does not say, or omits a qualification that changes its meaning. Give the location, the cited
   work, and what the source actually says (with page or section if you can).
3. **Prior work that already establishes the contribution.** Published or circulated work that
   already contains the paper's main result or its claimed increment. Give full bibliographic
   details and say exactly which claim it anticipates. Only name works you are confident exist;
   state your confidence. Do not guess at content you have not read.

For each defect, say how serious it is (fatal to the main claim / requires a change to the main
claim / local fix) and what minimal change would repair it.

If you find no defects of a kind, say so explicitly. Reply in plain text, numbered.
