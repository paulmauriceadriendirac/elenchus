## How these were produced

An external reviewer read the three PDFs cold, then independently re-derived the formal results and re-ran the numerical and combinatorial claims from scratch (Python; no access to the authors' code at the time of writing). Highlights:

- **A6.** Recomputed the two-error Gaussian model of Bjorn: 0.4169 / 0.3557 / 0.3669 / 0.9500, decline thresholds 1.120c / 0.936c / 0.687c, values −0.0496 / −0.1207 / −0.2737; the 1.12c threshold is exact. The three §5.2 worked examples check to three decimals.
- **A5.** Brute-forced all 256 (three-world) and 65,536 (four-world) belief states: the 4.30 and 4.94 optima are unique and correct; the four-world closure failure, the 7.25 / 6.91 refinement values, and the Dorst–Mandelkern figures all reproduce.
- **A4.** Re-derived the three witness profiles and the two identity theorems; the additive gain identities hold; the 355-preorder count reproduces exactly (5 calibrated, 2 total preorders). The Halpern (2015) quotations and page numbers are accurate against arXiv:1505.00162v1.

After the reports were drafted, they were cross-checked against this repository's own `state/audit/` outputs. The two sets of results agree on every number; the audits are stronger than the reports in two places (the A4 corollary is verified by exact SMT over all anonymous Pareto filters and all real additive scores; the A6 structural results are exhaustive over all 594 structures), and the reports catch one wording error the audits also flagged but which survives in the submitted PDF (A4 p. 6, "In componentwise terms", below).

**One correction to an earlier draft of the A5 report.** It initially flagged the footnote-2 counts "28 / 216" as an error. The rendered PDF reads **2⁸** and **2¹⁶**; the counts are correct and the finding was a text-extraction artefact (pdftotext drops superscripts). The report now says so.

## The three or four things most worth responding to

1. **A4, p. 6 — "In componentwise terms, the escape indexes the preorder by variable."** A componentwise filter, as §2 defines it, has one preorder applied to every variable; a preorder indexed by variable is not componentwise in that sense — which is precisely what makes the escape an escape. The sentence contradicts itself and would let a reader think the paper's own class can do something the paper has just proved it cannot. Wording only; no theorem affected. Suggested: "In variable-indexed componentwise terms…".
2. **A4, p. 4 — "Suzy's throw is a cause exactly when each switch-setting is part of a cause."** Which notion of "part of a cause" is in play (single candidate vs. conjunctive witness), and over which class of preorders? The equivalence is probably right on the natural reading, but the reader cannot tell which reading is intended.
3. **A5 — the state-indexed family version of Theorem 2.** Dorst's measures are indexed to the current state; the paper asserts that ME-at-each-member plus cross-family BI yields fixed thresholds "without collapsing the family into one separable measure", in two sentences. This is the version of the claim that matters dialectically; it should be stated and proved.
4. **A6, §6.3 — the relativized Bjorn bet.** The paper notes that relativizing normality to "how much does Bjorn weigh" yields a Goldstein–Hawthorne-like model in which current knowledge is not sensitive to future observations, and says "I have not computed the bet there." Since the headline claim is anchored on Bjorn, this is the main open spot.

## Questions for the authors

- A4: was the variable-indexed escape intended to be outside the "componentwise" class, and if so, what is the right label for it? Does the SMT check in `state/audit/A4/formal/` cover it?
- A4: can the Halpern 2016 pinpoints (84–85, 121, 133–134, 149–150, §3.3) be verified from a copy? They are unverifiable in the repository's citation audit and in the external review.
- A5: does the family version of the theorem go through for a family not generated by a single measure? If so, is the argument short enough to include?
- A5: is the record premium best defended as a component of accuracy, or as a separate epistemic good that accuracy maximization trades against? The reports press the first reading because that is what §4.2 needs.
- A6: is there a computation of the Bjorn bet under the weight-relative normality of §6.3?
- A6: in the Dorst et al. (2021) comparison, how are ties in the expert's state-indexed probabilities handled in "KDT with the fixed tie order is a recommended strategy"?
